# Playbooks

Playbooks are agent-followable recipes distilled from successful work. Their purpose is to
teach the agent to **generate a plan** — assess current state, identify gaps, produce a
prioritised action plan — not to follow a fixed sequence of steps blindly. A playbook is a
thinking scaffold, not a script.
