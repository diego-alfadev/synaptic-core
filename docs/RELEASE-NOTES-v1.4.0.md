# Synaptic v1.4.0

**Turn your daily work into a brain your agent can actually use. Plain files. Zero runtime. Any agent.**

This is the biggest Synaptic release yet — and if you're still on a bare v1.0, everything below lands in
**one clean `/synaptic-upgrade`** (additive, no schema change, nothing breaks).

**Coming from a bare v1.0? Buckle up. It just got serious.**

---

## 🌐 One brain, wherever you work — REACH
- Your brain follows you across **every** folder, or stays scoped to one. You decide by *where it's wired* — not where the files sit.
- **Convert a project brain to global** (or back) — guided, crash-safe, byte-for-byte, rolls back if anything looks off.
- Every `init` / `upgrade` now **detects how your brain is wired and fixes it** — no more "works in this folder, invisible in the next."

## ⚡ Value in 10 minutes — onboarding that doesn't hurt
- **Day-1 walkthrough:** empty brain → first note → first retrieval → your first *"if I vanished tomorrow, here's everything"* handover. Ten minutes, no jargon.
- **`/synaptic-handover`:** a new-joiner / vacation brief straight from the brain, on demand — what it is, the key decisions, the open threads, where to look. The knowledge tax, gone.
- Commands now show up in your agent's **`/` menu, with descriptions.** Stop hunting.
- **A path for non-developers** — managers and POs get the value without touching a terminal.

## 🎯 Capture you can trust
- A **structured session protocol** — Goal · Discoveries · Done · Next · Files. Signal, not a wall of text.
- A **"did capture actually fire?" audit** that catches silently-dead hooks. Never open your brain to an empty journal again.

## 🧭 Know what's alive — and what to trust
- **`lifecycle`** (project · area · resource · dormant) **+ `status`** (active · stale · archived): two dials, one grep, instant answers to *"what's live right now?"* and *"can I trust this?"* Archive, don't delete. Bloat, beaten.

## 🕸️ See your brain
- A real **interactive, force-directed graph** — zoom, filter, search, expand. Not a static blob.
- **God-node & surprising-edge audits** — the over-connected hubs, and the links that shouldn't exist.

## 🛡️ Governance, in the box
- A crisp **local-vs-remote data boundary** (code stays 100% local; prose goes to your host LLM) — drop-in text for regulated environments.

---

## The one thing that never changes
It's **just files.** Zero runtime. Any agent. Git-diffable. Portable. Yours.
No daemon, no lock-in, no gigabyte download — offline, air-gapped, and ready for whatever agent you're using next year.

## 🚀 …and the future? We're not telling you everything.
- Retrieval is about to get **fast, and smart** — without ever taking your files hostage. Opt-in. Switchable. Local.
- Your brain is getting a **memory that lives and breathes** — hot working-memory feeding your cold, curated knowledge. A brain that gets *better* with use, not just bigger.
- We've left the breadcrumbs in the repo. The curious will find them.
- One promise that doesn't break: **whatever ships next, it stays just files underneath.**
